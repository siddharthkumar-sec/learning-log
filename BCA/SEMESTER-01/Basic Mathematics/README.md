## Learning Objectives

Through this subject, I aim to:

- Understand fundamental mathematical concepts and definitions.
- Develop logical and analytical reasoning skills.
- Learn set theory, matrices, and mathematical logic.
- Understand algebraic structures and graph theory.
- Develop knowledge of statistics and data analysis.
- Improve accuracy and confidence in mathematical problem-solving.

## Study Approach

**LEARN → UNDERSTAND → SOLVE → VERIFY → REVISE**

My approach is to understand mathematical concepts step by step, practise different types of problems, verify solutions, and strengthen problem-solving skills through regular revision.
