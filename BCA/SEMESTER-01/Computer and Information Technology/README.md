## Learning Objectives

Through this subject, I aim to:

- Understand computer fundamentals and basic concepts of IT.
- Develop knowledge of operating systems and their functions.
- Learn about data communication and computer networks.
- Understand number systems and Boolean logic.
- Build a foundation in programming concepts and the World Wide Web.

## Study Approach

**LEARN → UNDERSTAND → PRACTICE → REVISE**

My approach is to understand each module through organized notes, practise sample questions, and revise important concepts to strengthen my fundamentals.
